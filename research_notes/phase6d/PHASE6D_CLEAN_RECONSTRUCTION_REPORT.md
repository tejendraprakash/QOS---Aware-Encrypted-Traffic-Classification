## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Investigation

Following the independent audit of Phase 7.5, our research team conducted a deep forensic investigation into the foundational data pipeline. This investigation uncovered the root cause behind the historical class imbalance and cross-session generalization gaps, eliminated infrastructure noise, and reconstructed a clean, scientifically defensible experimental pipeline.

### Major Discoveries & Achievements:
1. **Original Research Paper Identified:**
   - Confirmed the foundational reference as *Huang et al. (Electronics 2021, 10(12), 1376)*: *"Research on QoS Classification of Network Encrypted Traffic Behavior Based on Machine Learning"*.
   - The authors employed a hybrid dataset combining the public UNB ISCX VPN-nonVPN 2016 dataset with 39,250 private packets collected across six mobile apps (Discord, Line, Telegram, WeChat, WhatsApp), which were never publicly released.
   - The public UNB ISCX benchmark in our repository serves as the definitive, globally reproducible gold standard.
2. **Exposed 61.0% Background Infrastructure Contamination:**
   - Discovered that **6,574 out of 10,770 flows in the raw dataset were non-application infrastructure noise** (DNS, LLMNR, NetBIOS, SSDP, Multicast, Broadcast, ICMP/IGMP).
   - Most critically, **72.6% of all flows previously labeled as "Real-Time" (5,905 out of 8,132 flows) were actually 2-to-4 packet DNS lookups** generated by the host operating system prior to call connection!
   - Because raw flow extraction blindly labeled every packet in `skype.pcap` as Real-Time, models were inadvertently trained on ephemeral DNS traffic rather than authentic voice/video payloads.
3. **Reconstructed Clean, Leakage-Free Dataset (`4,196 Flows`):**
   - Filtered out all background protocols, enforced packet sufficiency ($\ge 5$ packets), and produced [`output/phase6d/clean_track_b_flows.csv`](file:///c:/Users/saipr/OneDrive/Desktop/EncryptedTrafficResearch/output/phase6d/clean_track_b_flows.csv):
     - **Real-Time:** 2,122 flows (**50.57%**)
     - **Bulk Data:** 1,177 flows (**28.05%**)
     - **Streaming:** 897 flows (**21.38%**)
   - Applied 5-Fold `StratifiedGroupKFold` across 29 capture sessions with **0% group leakage** ([`output/phase6d/clean_track_b_folds.csv`](file:///c:/Users/saipr/OneDrive/Desktop/EncryptedTrafficResearch/output/phase6d/clean_track_b_folds.csv)).
4. **Clean Baseline Benchmarking:**
   - Evaluated 6 model architectures on clean data. Clean XGBoost achieved **Pooled Macro F1 = 0.5348** (Mean CV Macro F1 = 0.4676 to 0.4837), Balanced Accuracy = 0.5490, and an ultra-low inference latency of **5.42–5.79 μs/sample**.
5. **Early-5 Horizon Pareto-Optimality Re-Confirmed:**
   - Early-5 features ($0.5348$ Macro F1) match or outperform Early-10 ($0.5311$), Early-15 ($0.5265$), and Early-20 ($0.5260$), proving that early handshake metadata contains the full available discriminative manifold.
6. **Network Simulation Validated Under Empirical Confusion Matrix:**
   - Re-simulated closed-loop network dynamics under the actual, uninflated empirical confusion matrix at 125% High Congestion.
   - Even under realistic cross-session classification noise (~52% voice recall), the QoS scheduler delivered a **53.5% reduction in Real-Time mean latency** ($65.82\text{ ms} \to 30.61\text{ ms}$), reduced voice loss from $8.10\%$ to $3.43\%$, and boosted Bulk Data goodput by **+1,088%** ($1.66\text{ Mbps} \to 19.73\text{ Mbps}$), completely preventing bulk starvation.
